## 摘要
预测一个已训练模型能否在分布偏移下泛化仍然很困难，尤其是在目标域数据不可用时。我们提出 STAMP（语义时序增强模型预测），这是一种仅使用源域、无需目标标签的准则，可从成对的源域图像估计分布外（OOD）性能。STAMP 通过对比语义稳定配对与随机配对来计算输出空间相关比 η²=S_B/S_T：η² 越高，表明模型输出随语义身份变化，而非随干扰变异变化。在涵盖 CNN、ViT、MetaFormer、基础模型以及 SSL/VLM 探针的 44 个胸部 X 射线模型上，时序 STAMP 在 VinDr-CXR、CheXpert 和 MIMIC-CXR 上与宏平均 AUROC 的 Spearman 相关系数达到 0.844–0.855；类别匹配变体将单类别 RSNA 从 0.311 提升至 0.663。STAMP 在仅使用源域的方法中取得最佳的平均医学排名，并在无需任何目标数据的情况下优于目标域 ATC 和 AoTL 估计器。在 27 个 ImageNet 模型上，温度缩放的 STAMPTS 在 ObjectNet 上达到 ρ=0.984，在另外四个分布偏移上达到 ρ≥0.905，在控制 ImageNet 准确率后偏相关系数为 0.662–0.949。在单块 GPU 上每个模型约需 12 秒，STAMP 是一种实用的部署前模型选择与审计工具。

## Abstract
Predicting whether a trained model will generalize under distribution shift remains difficult, especially when target-domain data are unavailable. We introduce STAMP (Semantic Temporal Augmented Model Prediction), a source-only, target-label-free criterion that estimates out-of-distribution (OOD) performance from paired source-domain images. STAMP computes the output-space correlation ratio $η^2=S_B/S_T$ by contrasting semantically stable pairs with random pairs: higher $η^2$ indicates that model outputs vary with semantic identity rather than nuisance variation. On 44 chest X-ray models spanning CNNs, ViTs, MetaFormers, foundation models, and SSL/VLM probes, temporal STAMP attains Spearman correlations of $0.844$--$0.855$ with macro AUROC on VinDr-CXR, CheXpert, and MIMIC-CXR; a class-matched variant improves single-class RSNA from $0.311$ to $0.663$. STAMP attains the best average source-only medical ranking and outperforms the target-domain ATC and AoTL estimators without any target data. On 27 ImageNet models, temperature-scaled STAMPTS attains $ρ{=}0.984$ on ObjectNet and $ρ\geq0.905$ on four additional distribution shifts, with partial correlations of $0.662$--$0.949$ after controlling for ImageNet accuracy. Requiring approximately 12s per model on one GPU, STAMP is a practical pre-deployment model-selection and auditing tool.
